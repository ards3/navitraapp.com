How it works

Getting from "where should we even start?" to a finished plan takes four steps — and most of them are fun.

Pick a city

Search any destination on Earth, or tell the assistant what kind of trip you're dreaming about — even a photo works. Navitra knows the highlights and the hidden corners.

01

Swipe what you love

Museums or markets, viewpoints or street food — swipe right on what excites you, left on what doesn't. Navitra learns your taste as you go.

02

AI builds your days

The route engine sequences every stop around opening hours, walking distances and your daily energy — then balances it across the days of your trip.

03

Explore, with stories

Follow the route with turn-by-turn directions, listen to voice guides as you arrive, and let it all work offline when the roaming bill says no.
